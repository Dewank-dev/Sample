Main file: index.html, plus send.html (a tool for me) and the asset files listed below.

4. THE DOORS (full-screen intro, shown on every visit)
==================================================
Lock page scroll while it shows; keep the page under it scrolled to the top and hide the hero until the reveal.

Look: two tall carved palace doors filling the screen, meeting in the middle. Deep red lacquer with an embossed mandala and paisley relief (CSS gradients or SVG with an emboss filter), gold borders, brass studs in a grid, and two brass ring handles. A marigold and mango-leaf toran hangs across the top: a scalloped garland of orange and yellow marigolds with green leaves (SVG). A small brass bell hangs in the middle of the toran. In the centre where the doors meet: a round gold medallion (the "seal") with the Om symbol or ganesha.png, and a lit diya below it with a flickering flame. Text: "TAP TO OPEN" with a small down chevron above the medallion (pulsing), and "Specially for" plus the guest name in large gold script below the diya. A Skip button (top right pill) appears only once the opening starts. A warm golden light wash layer sits on top, invisible at first.

Tapping anywhere starts the opening (the Escape key skips). Use the Web Animations API, timers and requestAnimationFrame. Times are measured from the tap:
- 0 ms: hide the hint and the name, show Skip, start opening.mp3. During this same tap, "unlock" music.mp3 and chime.mp3 for phones by playing each one muted and pausing it at once, so they can play later. The diya flame grows and glows brighter.
- 0 to 1700 ms: a light runs around the medallion, 1.5 turns, clockwise from 12 o'clock, ease-in-out. Draw it as an SVG comet just outside the medallion edge: three circles with pathLength=100 and dash patterns "3.5 96.5", "11 89" and "26 74" (white, pale gold, gold), sharing one leading edge (stroke-dashoffset = dash length minus progress), with a gold glow filter. A trail ring draws itself behind it until it is a full glowing circle. Emit small gold sparks from the comet head every 50 ms.
- 1600 ms: the brass bell on the toran swings (rotate 18 degrees back and forth, slowing down) and rings. The medallion flashes and sparks burst out.
- 1800 to 3200 ms: the toran lights up flower by flower from the centre outwards (each marigold glows and pulses), and the mango leaves sway.
- 2200 ms: a wave of gold light spreads out from the centre across both doors, lighting up the mandala and paisley relief as it passes (a gold copy of the pattern revealed by a growing radial ring mask, opacity 0 -> 0.8 -> 0 over 2 s), with star twinkles.
- 3800 to 5800 ms: the doors swing open. Rotate the left door to -105 degrees and the right door to 105 degrees around their outer edges, with perspective, cubic-bezier(.55,0,.3,1). Bright golden light pours out from the gap: a vertical light beam and slowly rotating rays (screen blend). Marigold petals (orange and yellow) and rose petals (red) burst out of the opening and drift down with gravity, sway and spin.
- 5600 ms: the light wash fades in over 0.6 s. Then hide the doors, show the hero, start the hero video, start music.mp3 with a gentle fade-in, zoom the hero from 0.93 to 1, fade the wash out over 1 s, remove the intro, and only then start the scroll reveals and the falling petals.
- Skip: jump straight to the wash (0.25 s), fade out opening.mp3 quickly, start the music, reveal the page.
- prefers-reduced-motion: a tap simply fades the doors out and starts the music. No sound effects.
